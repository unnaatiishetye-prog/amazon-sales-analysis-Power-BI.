# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes Amazon sales data to uncover
revenue trends, top-performing products, category contribution, and
state-wise profit.

![Dashboard Preview](images/dashboard_preview.png)

## 🎯 Objective
To help business teams understand sales performance and identify the products,
categories, months, and regions that drive revenue and profit.

## 📌 Key Metrics
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | 6.25M |
| Total Profit | 1.57M |
| Total Quantity Sold | 818 |
| Profit Margin | 25.20% |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Electronics dominates**, contributing 74.33% (4.64M) of total revenue.
- **Laptops and Smartphones** are the top two revenue-generating products.
- **March is the peak sales month**, with a smaller spike in October.
- **Maharashtra leads in profit (0.39M)**, followed by Gujarat (0.24M).
- Home & Kitchen (10.71%) and Fashion (8.43%) are the next largest categories.

## 📈 Dashboard Visuals
- KPI cards: Revenue, Profit, Quantity Sold, Profit Margin %
- Line chart: Total Revenue by Month
- Bar chart: Total Revenue by Product
- Donut chart: Total Revenue by Category
- Area chart: Total Profit by State

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- Power BI Desktop
- DAX (measures and calculations)
- Excel / CSV (data source)
